VISION STATEMENT

Provision of relevant and quality education for students to enable them acquire knowledge, skills and values to make them functionally literate and to pursue higher education.

MISSION STATEMENT

*Providing a safe, happy school in which pastoral care is given high priority by dedicated staff.
*Giving children an excellent, secure start to their school experience in the Kindergarten.
*Ensuring effective teaching and learning.
*Empowering the learner to cope with both success and failure.
*Ensuring that teachers are abreast with current policies and teaching methods.
*Encouraging parents to have a voice in the running of the school.

CORE VALUES
